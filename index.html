How to Play

Whisker Woods is one single winding path with 6 levels on it, in order. You can't skip ahead — each creature physically blocks the trail until you beat it.

  • Move: Arrow keys or WASD
  • Interact: Enter / Space (use it on the Camp or Portal tiles)
  • Pause: Esc, any time you're walking

The 6 levels, in order:

  • 1. Slime — Memory Trial: flip cards, find every pair
  • 2. Bat — Reflex Trial: click each pest before it ducks away
  • 3. Crab — Rhythm Trial: watch the pattern, then repeat it
  • 4. Golem — Reflex Trial: click each pest before it ducks away
  • 5. Ghost — a longer Memory Trial
  • 6. Dragon — the final, longest Rhythm Trial

Winning grows your cat and unlocks new cats to play as — press Enter on the Camp tile any time to pick one. Beat all 6 and the Portal opens, but it needs fuel: catch fruit (not bugs!) across 3 short levels to power up and reach the next world.

Whisker Woods

Deep in Whisker Woods, six guardians stand along the only path to the Ancient Portal. Legend says any cat brave enough to out-puzzle all six will grow into a Legendary Cat.

Move with Arrow Keys or WASD. Walk into a guardian to challenge it — there's no way around them, only through.
